The Story

A Word in a Prayer Room

I got saved in 1996 and filled with the Holy Spirit with the evidence of speaking in tongues the following year.

On July 23rd, 2002, in a place of prayer, God said to me:

Just as Abraham delivered Lot, Moses the children of Israel, and Daniel did the same, I am raising friends in this generation and the next generation who will deliver my people from mediocrity, poverty and shame, and you are one of them.

I was young, and the words were larger than my life. I wrote them down and carried them the way you carry a promise you cannot yet explain.

Two years later, on November 19th, 2004, I was talking to God. Now, I had just listened to a message by a beloved brother titled The Six-Day Creation Package, in which he revealed how everyone's assignment is connected to a day of creation. I did not know mine. So I asked the Lord which day my assignment was tied to, and I heard, “God said…”

God said? I asked. And the Lord answered:

know What I am saying, say it unto them.

I still did not understand, and He did not leave me there. He opened my understanding with these words:

Your assignment has nothing to do with any day, but with the Word that created every day. And as you keep speaking what I tell you over their lives, I will keep creating new things for them, as I did in the beginning.

On January 4th, 2006, the Upper Room Business was launched, a prayer room ordained to birth the vision of House of Joy and to guard it, so that what God had promised would not suffer a spiritual miscarriage before it was ever born. In that room I saw miracles, signs and wonders, and the manifestation of spiritual gifts.

And still, in the middle of all of it, I was hungry for an understanding. I felt there was more to all I knew that guaranteed certainty in many areas.

On November 22nd, 2010, the answer came. God opened my eyes to see a vision of the beginning, and there I saw it: the first word man ever heard was the blessing. Before man had done anything, before there was any striving to earn or any shame to hide, God had already spoken a blessing over him. That was the beginning I had been searching for.

And it was revealed to me that the core of my message, the thing my whole life had been reaching toward, was to preach Christ, the Blessing. For Christ is called the Blessing because every blessing God has for man is wrapped up in Him; He is the fullness of God's blessing to man, and everything the Father ever intended to give is found in the Son. The hunger finally had a name.

Everything after that was the promise becoming a place. In July 2014, I was ordained into the ministry. In April 2016, House of Joy Church Worldwide opened its doors for its first service. What God had whispered in a prayer room in 2002 had, over fourteen years, become a house where people could come and hear it for themselves.

And the house was never meant to hold it. Out of that same word came GIMA, the Gospel Invasion Mandate, carrying one mission: to spread the gospel of Christ across the nations of the earth. Its flagship campaign went out from Kaduna on November 10th, 2017, and from that day it has moved from city to city, carrying the Blessing beyond our walls to whoever will hear.

I preach Christ, not as a concept but as the Blessing Himself, until mediocrity, poverty, and shame lose their grip on every life that hears.

So welcome. Whatever brought you here, you did not arrive by accident. Everything on this site, the Messages, the Articles, the Poems, and the Daily Victory Confession, has been prepared to feed your spirit and take you from one level of glory to another. Explore it freely, return to it often, and let the Word that created every day begin to create something new in yours.

What We Hold

Statement of Faith

The Scriptures

We believe the Bible is the inspired and infallible Word of God, given for doctrine, reproof, correction, and instruction in righteousness. It is our final authority in all things. 2 Timothy 3:16

The Godhead

We believe in one God, eternally existing as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, the Maker of heaven and earth and of all things visible and invisible. Matthew 28:19

The Lord Jesus Christ

We believe in the deity of the Lord Jesus Christ, His virgin birth, His sinless life, His atoning death, His bodily resurrection, and His soon return in glory. John 1:1,14 · 1 Corinthians 15:3-4

Salvation

We believe salvation is by grace through faith in the finished work of Christ alone, not of works, and that whosoever calls upon the name of the Lord shall be saved. Ephesians 2:8-9 · Romans 10:13

The Believer's Inheritance

We believe every believer is blessed with all spiritual blessings in Christ, called out of mediocrity, poverty, and shame into a glorious inheritance in Him. Ephesians 1:3,18 · Galatians 3:13-14

The Holy Spirit and the Church

We believe in the person and power of the Holy Spirit at work in the believer, and in the church as the body of Christ, gathered to worship, grow, and carry the gospel to the world. Acts 1:8 · Ephesians 4:11-13

Whatever brought you here today, I want you to know that God is not finished with you. The circumstances around you do not have the final say over your life. Christ does. And in Him there is no mediocrity, no poverty, and no shame, only an inheritance waiting to be walked in.

So let me pray for you. May the eyes of your understanding be opened. May you see what heaven says about you. And may everything you decree in faith be established, in Jesus' name.
